Our work toward PAGI (Pseudo-AGI) is open source and ongoing. [PacoMind](https://github.com/Aevonix/PacoMind) is part of that research: built for Hermes, it stores agent memory, identity and shared work independently of the models it uses, with support for local inference.

### HPC equipment and operations

We work with OEMs and resellers to help customers gain priority access to HPC equipment we sell. We also support datacenter placement, cluster management and monetization through our partners and network.

We have supported several NVIDIA B300 deployments across Central America and the United States.
